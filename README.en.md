## About me

Inter Cel is the product I have worked on most extensively and continue to work on today. I develop backend systems and integrations with the telecom operator Vivo across several stages of the customer journey:

- Purchasing physical SIM cards and eSIMs.
- Activating accounts and mobile services.
- Mobile top-ups and subscription flows.
- Connecting the frontend and backend for these journeys.
